## zed_snapshot source (for reproducibility)
`CMakeLists.txt`: `find_package(ZED REQUIRED)`, `find_package(CUDA
${ZED_CUDA_VERSION} REQUIRED)`, link `${ZED_LIBRARIES}` and CUDA
cudart/cuda. `zed_snapshot.cpp`: `sl::Camera::open` (HD720, 30 fps,
PERFORMANCE, METER), 90× `grab()`, `retrieveImage(LEFT)`,
`retrieveImage(VIEW::DEPTH)`, `retrieveMeasure(DEPTH)`, `sl::Mat::write` to
PNG, then luma and depth statistics sampled every 4th pixel. The full source
is on the Orin at `~/zed_install/zed_snapshot/`.

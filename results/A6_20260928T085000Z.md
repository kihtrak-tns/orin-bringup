## Checks beyond "exited cleanly"
- **Motion content** (odom decoded from the bags):
  - static: extent x 0.08, y 0.11, z 0.06 m. Stationary; the ~4 m
    summed path is pose jitter integrated at ~26 Hz, not movement.
  - hand-carried: extent **x 1.83, y 1.52, z 0.86 m**, summed path
    16.9 m, ending (−0.12, −0.33, 0.06) m from the start. A real
    walked lap with lifting, returning near the start.
- **Gaps > 1.2 s**, located in time:
  - static, `/laksa/state` (and the other ESP32 topics) **1.83 s at
    ~26.6 s** in = a micro-ROS **session reset at 08:34:21Z** (agent
    journal, 27.8 s after the recording started). This is the known open
    instability (`notes/microros_link_stability.md`), not a recorder
    problem.
  - `/laksa/pca9685/state` (nominal 1 Hz): repeated ~2.0 s gaps in both bags
    (skipped samples; effective 0.84–0.91 Hz) and one 4.0 s gap at ~18.9 s
    in the hand-carried bag. Independent of session resets. Minor, but
    anything replaying steering state should not assume a steady 1 Hz.
  - The hand-carried window had **no** session reset; its ESP32 gaps are
    ≤ 0.46 s.
- **Session churn in the whole window** (08:33–08:49Z): 4 resets (08:34:21,
  08:39:09, 08:43:44, 08:45:48). That's more frequent than earlier in the night,
  with the Orin under heavy load (ZED neural depth + tracking + bag writes).
  Worth feeding into the open link investigation.

## Not recorded / caveats
- **`/laksa/brake` is absent from both bags.** It is in the core topic list,
  but nothing publishes it, and rosbag2 (Humble) only subscribes to topics
  that exist, so it has 0 messages and doesn't even appear in
  `metadata.yaml`. Expected for listen-only recording. When a command
  publisher exists (Phase C+), the bag will pick it up.
- **`/laksa/imu/data` ≈ 24–25 Hz** in both bags, against ~41 Hz in the
  firmware findings (and ~28 Hz in A5's loaded run). With the Orin heavily
  loaded, this looks like a real reduction in delivered rate, not a recorder
  artefact. Open question for the micro-ROS link work.
- **ZED image rates** 12–16 Hz (bagged ≈ live), same caveat as A5.
- **No TF recorded.** `/tf` and `/tf_static` are not in the topic list. For
  Phase E replay/mapping, add `/tf_static` (ZED camera frames) and decide
  whether to record the ZED's `/tf` or recompute it. A launch-file
  follow-up, deliberately not changed tonight.
- Bags are sqlite3. At ~105 MB/s the writer kept up (live and bagged rates
  match), but MCAP or image compression is worth considering for longer
  runs.

## Verdict
FAIL at B4. With nothing connected, pin 15 reads HIGH, so a loose or disconnected signal wire reports RUN.
Root cause: Jetson.GPIO 2.1.9 ignores pull_up_down ("Jetson.GPIO ignores setup()'s pull_up_down parameter"). The pin has no working pull-down; the earlier "disconnected -> 0" bench read does not hold.
Fix is hardware: external pull-down, ~10 kΩ from pin 15 to GND (the Rx drives it through push-pull, so 10k is fine). Alternative: a pinmux/DT pull-down. Then re-run B1–B7 in full.
